# Renpet festival calendars

Renpet offers four selectable, locally packaged festival catalogs separate
from user-created appointments.

| Source | Contents | Date rules |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `amun-theban` | Selected Theban Amun observances | Egyptian fixed dates |
| `esna-khnum` | Selected Esna observances | Egyptian fixed dates |
| `isis-philae` | Selected Isis/Philae observances | Egyptian fixed dates |
| `national-egypt` | Egyptian national and civic occasions | Source Gregorian or astronomical date |

Only the Amun catalog is enabled initially. Source selection does not depend
on the optional Gregorian date overlay or the chosen week-grid presentation.
The available catalogs are the complete supported set; a missing or malformed
catalog is an error, not a trigger to load another catalog.

## Dates and explanatory information

Ancient festival dates are displayed on the modern Alexandrian calendar. They
are modern date projections, not evidence that the Gregorian occurrence stayed
fixed throughout Egyptian history. Festival details include a short explanation
and source references when available, rather than full academic commentary.

Sham El Nessim belongs to the Egyptian national catalog. Its observance is
computed as the Monday after Coptic Orthodox Easter; that calculation is only
a date rule, not a separate Christian festival catalog. National anniversaries
are not automatically moved to the dates of announced public holidays.

Catalog identifiers remain stable across Egyptian years; the year number
belongs in calendar presentation, not an event identifier. All visible
festival text is authored in Arabic, Coptic and hieroglyphic presentation.
The software does not claim that every modern editorial translation is an
attested ancient Egyptian quotation.
